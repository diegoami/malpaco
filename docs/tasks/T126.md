# T126 Start and phase announcements

- **Kind**: owner-requested pre-0.3.0 clarity; #322, parent #271 / release #319.
- **Owns**: player-game announcement view/timer, fresh-start lifecycle binding, relevant host presentation and tests.
- **Scope**: Present a large centered translucent banner over the map, with a fresh-game three-second countdown and clear turn/phase messages such as “Purple is moving” and “Your turn to move”. Use the public seat colours and human phase guidance. A fresh start counts 3, 2, 1 before enabling play; retained games Resume directly, without restarting a countdown. Human cards, placement, attack, capture and friendly movement each have meaningful instructions. Announcements time out after three unpaused seconds, do not intercept map input afterward and never acknowledge cards/world notices or confirm troop amounts. Persistent existing guidance remains after the banner goes away. These durations are designed UI choices, not rule constants. Pauses, native background and teardown cannot accidentally resume play or fire stale callbacks. Respect reduced motion and narrow/short viewports. AI policy remains unchanged.
- **Done when**:
  1. Controlled-clock checks prove fresh countdown order and input gating, followed by the human instruction; loading a retained match skips countdown and retains saved identity/history/RNG.
  2. Actual human phase transitions and displayed computer seat/turn changes show the matching announcement. Repeated renders do not restart it; it disappears at its deadline. No late previous-seat announcement can overwrite the current one.
  3. Pause/background freezes or hides announcements and preserves explicit Resume. Teardown cancels callbacks. Timing never applies a game action, dismisses a mandatory choice or rewrites a saved match.
  4. Pinned screenshots at phone portrait, short landscape and desktop show large, centered, partly transparent text without clipping; after countdown the banner permits map interaction. New behavioral tests fail before and pass after; build/types/units, affected browser specs and exact-head CI pass before merge.
