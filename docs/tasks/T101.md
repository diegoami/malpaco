- **Scope**:
  - **Every floating card folds and drags** (attack, capture, friendly move, sets, discard, reward, Raid confirm, result):
    - a fold control shrinks the card to a small chip that keeps its title; tapping the chip unfolds it; folding never changes the game;
    - the card drags by its edge or a grip (pointer and touch); it stays inside the safe viewport; taps on its buttons never start a drag;
    - the dragged position is kept for later cards in this browser until a reset (double-tap the grip, or a new game);
    - a drag or fold never swallows a map tap outside the card (A11).
  - **The game history leaves the player's UI**: no history icon in the menu, no History on the result card, no "Changes since your last turn" notice action. It stays reachable only through a hidden link: `?log` on the URL shows the history icon again (tests and bug reports).
  - **Computer turns are quick and quiet**:
    - one notice per computer turn ("Orange's turn"), not one per action; no staged pair, outlines or names for computer actions, only the counts and colours changing;
    - at Normal speed a full round of three computers takes at most about 12 s on the reference seed (today about 50 s); Slow and Very slow stay for those who want to watch;
    - during a computer turn the only controls on screen are the speed controls: Pause, Fast-forward and the speed choice (a corner control, not inside the menu). The menu, Fit, Focus and Countries icons hide until the human's turn or until the game is paused; pausing brings them back.
- **Done when**:
  1. `npx playwright test tests/e2e/card-fold-drag.spec.ts tests/e2e/computer-turns.spec.ts --workers=2`, at 390×844, 844×390 and 1280×800. It must show:
     - each card type folds to a chip and unfolds, with the saved match unchanged by the engine oracle;
     - a card dragged by mouse and by touch moves, stays inside the viewport, keeps its place for the next card and after a reload, and a tap on its button does not drag;
     - a map tap outside a folded or dragged card reaches the map;
     - no history control in the menu, the notices or the result card; with `?log` the history icon is back and opens the log;
     - during a computer turn the visible controls are exactly Pause, Fast-forward and the speed choice; when paused, the menu icon returns;
     - one notice per computer turn;
     - a computer round at Normal on seed 7 finishes within 12 s of real time, with the same actions as before (speed changes waiting only).
  2. `npm test && npm run build` pass; existing specs migrate without weakening an assertion.
- **Hazards**:
  - **Speed never changes play.** The step times change waiting only: the actions, RNG and history stay identical (existing playback tests check this).
  - **Drag versus tap.** Use a movement threshold, so a tap on Fight or Confirm is never a drag.
  - **Stale controls.** Hidden controls still carry their revision checks.
