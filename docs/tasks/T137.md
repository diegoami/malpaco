- **Kind**: #341/#334 tactical slice after T135/T136; issue#358,branch `task/T137-offensive-candidate`; arena-only,manual review before adoption.
- **Owns**: `computer-offensive.ts`,explicit stopping option in positioning,defense-only shared-evaluator option,core exports,arena adapter,tactical tests/docs. Dependencies remain separate PRs.
- **Scope**: Rank a bounded shortlist of legal strict-advantage attack campaigns against stopping (including a legal movement). Use T136's complete joint terminal intervals,not army gap or capture chance alone. Reconstruct each terminal public board; captured positions use allocation-v1's predicted choice/default,then assess source/new-front conditional exposure. Reevaluate after every actual exchange/capture via the adapter. [Designed] utility:one unit per retained country,two income turns,one per enemy casualty,minus one per own casualty,two for first capture's card when enabled,four for eliminating a seat; subtract largest conditional excess exposure,never sum alternative threats. Immediate domination/conquest probability precedes utility; conservative lower expected utility beats stopping,which wins ties. Frozen attack wins ties among attacks. Shortlist six pairs including frozen choice; prioritize elimination,targets killable within remaining exchanges,income swing,weakest defender,strongest source,IDs. Designed bounds:96 countries,256 projected positions,1048576 total model cells,existing constituent caps and eight enemy sources. Pre-admit calls by their maximum work so total cannot overshoot; all unsafe/unsupported/unknown or exhausted work falls back explicitly. Keep allocation-v1/movement-v1/default choices unchanged. No deeper search,combined-army attack,hidden information,rule/save/RNG/cap/UI change or personalities.
