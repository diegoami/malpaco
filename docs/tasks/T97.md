# T97 Bare-minimum floating cards and a trimmed menu

- **Kind**: feature (0.2.1)
- **Issue**: #246 · **Branch**: the session branch · **Merge after**: T96
- **Review**: none per PR; the 0.2.1 milestone review covers it.
- **Evidence**: owner decisions on #240 and #244 (2026-10-22); [designed].
- **Owns**: `src/ui/battleChrome.ts`, `src/ui/battleView.ts`, `src/ui/friendlyMoveView.ts`, `src/ui/cardView.ts`, `src/ui/victoryView.ts`, `src/ui/referenceBattleGame.ts`, `src/ui/mapCamera.ts` (obstacle registration only), `src/ui/help.ts`, `src/ui/palette.ts`, `src/ui/countryPicker.ts`, `src/ui/gameEventLog.ts`, the map views (accessible names), `src/styles.css`, `docs/play.md`, `tools/release/PLAY.md`, `tests/e2e/minimal-cards.spec.ts`, and the existing specs the change makes wrong.
- **Scope**:
  - **Cards float over the map.** No card, folded strip or notice reframes the camera; only the corner icons are obstacles. A card sits in a bottom corner and switches sides when it would cover the staged countries.
  - **Attack card: Fight and Cancel only.** Fight rolls one exchange per press. Fight on, Stop, the stop-point setting, the odds line and the pair line go.
  - **Capture card:** −, amount, +, Confirm. **Friendly move:** −, amount, +, Confirm, Cancel. No preview or range text.
  - **Card choices:**
    - sets: a button per legal set, plus Skip;
    - discard: a button per card;
    - reward: Continue;
    - Raid: its strip, then Confirm.

    No hand chips or long sentences.
  - **Notices:** keep the turn line, the computer notice with Fast-forward, and refusals. Drop "Choice folded" and the outcome lines.
  - **Icons:** the Information icon goes, and its objective moves into Help.
  - **Menu:** Pause and speed, Help & icon names, New game, and the game history behind a smaller icon. The history card closes by ×, Escape or a swipe. Totals, card counts and panel detail go.
  - **Countries list:** behind its icon only; it never opens by itself or stacks.
  - **Computer turns show no buttons** (owner, 2026-10-22): while a computer seat plays, no phase action, card or choice control is on screen; only the corner icons, Pause and the computer notice with Fast-forward stay.
  - **Seats are named by colour** (owner, 2026-10-22): the computers are Orange, Green and Purple after their seat colours (`src/ui/palette.ts`), and the human seat is You, everywhere a seat is named (notices, history, standings, the Countries list and the map's accessible names). A presentation name only: saves and the engine keep `Seat N`.
- **Done when**:
  1. `npx playwright test tests/e2e/minimal-cards.spec.ts --workers=2`, at 390×844, 844×390, 820×1180 and 1280×800:
     - opening each card leaves the map's viewBox unchanged;
     - the attack card holds exactly Fight and Cancel;
     - one Fight press is one exchange, by the engine oracle;
     - each other card holds only the controls listed in Scope;
     - a card never covers the staged pair's badges;
     - the history card closes by ×, by Escape and by a swipe;
     - no Information icon;
     - every remaining control is at least 44 px;
     - during a computer turn no button is visible outside the corner icons and the computer notice;
     - the computer seats read Orange, Green and Purple in the notice, the history and the result standings.
  2. `npm test && npm run build` pass; existing specs are migrated to the new controls without weakening what they check (the engine oracles stay).
- **Hazards**:
  - **Removing Fight on removes timers.** Keep the pause and stale-control gates on Fight, with the same revision checks.
  - **A card over the map must not swallow map taps outside its own rectangle** (A11).
