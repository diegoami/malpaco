# T104 See your cards: a hand button, and the hand after each draw

- **Kind**: feature (0.2.1)
- **Issue**: #263 · **Branch**: the session branch · **Merge after**: T103
- **Review**: none per PR; the 0.2.1 milestone review (#253) covers it, on the moved candidate.
- **Evidence**: the owner, 2026-10-22, playing the candidate: "I do not have a way to see my cards, there must be a button to show the cards, and also after the end of the turn the list of cards should be shown." T97 hid the hand from the screen (kept only for assistive technology); this brings it back on request. [designed] throughout.
- **Owns**: `src/ui/cardView.ts`, `src/ui/cardController.ts` (projection only), `src/ui/battleChrome.ts`, `src/ui/referenceBattleGame.ts`, `src/ui/icons.ts`, `src/ui/help.ts`, `src/styles.css`, `docs/play.md`, `tools/release/PLAY.md`, `docs/releases/0.2.1/ACCEPTANCE.md` (a line), `tests/e2e/hand.spec.ts`, and the existing specs the change makes wrong.
- **Scope**:
  - **A Cards button** in the corner icons, with the number of cards in your hand on it (hidden while you hold none, and during computer turns like the other icons). It opens a small card listing your cards by name, in hand order, and closes by ×, Escape or a tap on the button again. It changes nothing in the game.
  - **After a draw** (the reward at the end of your turn), the card shows the card you drew and then your whole hand, the new card marked, above Continue.
  - Only your own hand: never another seat's cards, and never a card before it is drawn (the discard rule, A6, stays).
  - Help names the Cards icon (without naming any card kind in the help text; a test checks that).
- **Done when**:
  1. `npx playwright test tests/e2e/hand.spec.ts --workers=2` at 390×844 and 1280×800, from fixtures with a known hand, shows:
     - the Cards button carries the hand count, opens the list with exactly the hand's cards in order, and closes by ×, Escape and a second tap; the saved match is unchanged (engine oracle);
     - with an empty hand the button is hidden, and during a computer turn it is hidden;
     - after a draw, the reward card lists the drawn card and the whole hand, the new card marked;
     - no other seat's cards appear anywhere on the page.
  2. `npm test && npm run build` pass; existing specs migrate without weakening an assertion.
- **Hazards**:
  - **Hidden information.** The view takes only the viewer's projection (`cardView` already does); never read `state.cards.hands` of another seat in the UI.
