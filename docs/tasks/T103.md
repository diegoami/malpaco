# T103 Guided taps: a country with no attack starts a move; a pulsing hint when you hesitate

- **Kind**: feature (0.2.1)
- **Issue**: #261 · **Branch**: the session branch · **Merge after**: the #260 fix
- **Review**: none per PR; the 0.2.1 milestone review (#253) covers it, on the moved candidate.
- **Evidence**: the owner, 2026-10-22, while playing the candidate: "clicking on a region from where you can attack should immediately prechoose fight. Choosing a region with no valid attack should prechoose move"; "Show the pulsing arrow after a delay when it seems that the player does not know what to do"; "Leave the button, but show a pulsing arrow if the user seems to hesitate". [designed] throughout.
- **Owns**: `src/ui/referenceBattleGame.ts`, `src/ui/battleChrome.ts`, `src/ui/battleView.ts`, `src/styles.css`, `src/ui/help.ts`, `docs/play.md`, `tools/release/PLAY.md`, `docs/releases/0.2.1/ACCEPTANCE.md` (a line), `tests/e2e/guided-taps.spec.ts`, and the existing specs the change makes wrong.
- **Scope**:
  - **In the attack phase, tapping one of your countries:**
    - with an enemy neighbour it can attack: picks it as the attacker, ready for a target and Fight (as now);
    - with no attack but a friendly move: starts the friendly move from it, as if Move armies were pressed first;
    - with neither: nothing is picked, and the one-line notice says why.
  - **A pulsing hint when you hesitate:** a small arrow pulses (grows and shrinks) next to the control you most likely need, only after a pause with no input during your own turn:
    - your armies are placed and combat has not started: it points at Start combat;
    - an attacker and a target are picked: it points at Fight;
    - a capture or a move waits for its amount: it points at Confirm.
    - It disappears at the next input of any kind, never shows during a computer turn or while paused, and it is presentation only (`aria-hidden`; the control keeps its own label).
  - The Start combat button stays.
- **Done when**:
  1. `npx playwright test tests/e2e/guided-taps.spec.ts --workers=2` at 390×844 and 1280×800 shows:
     - a tap on a country with an attack picks it as the attacker; a tap on a country with no attack but a friendly move enters the move with it as source; one with neither picks nothing; the saved match is unchanged by all of these (engine oracle);
     - after the delay with no input, the arrow is visible and points at Start combat, at Fight, and at Confirm in the three cases, and is gone after the next tap, during a computer turn and while paused;
     - before the delay, no arrow.
  2. `npm test && npm run build` pass; existing specs migrate without weakening an assertion.
- **Hazards**:
  - **The delay is [designed]** (about 4 s); tests use the page clock, never real waiting.
  - **Never act for the player.** The tap only picks; nothing rolls or moves armies without the player's own Fight or Confirm.
