- **Scope**: Both maps take the sketches' look: vivid owner regions (blue, orange, lime, purple) over a dark sea; a layered shoreline (dark shelf, light coast); thin dotted routes; small dark count badges outlined in the owner's colour, shaped square, diamond, hexagon or circle by owner; a gold source badge and outline, dashed outlines on legal targets, and dimmed unrelated countries while a country is chosen. Country and island names show only on hover, focus or selection; at rest the map shows counts only. Out of scope: land geometry (#181), flavoured names (T94), the annotation and leader work of T87.

  2. `npm test && npm run build` — Pass; CI's full browser suite is green.
- **Hazards**: Counts never shrink below 14 px or hide. Label paint never intercepts hits. Colour is never the only ownership cue. Core, RNG and saves are untouched.
