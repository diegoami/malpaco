- **Scope**: Add one basic pure deterministic policy returning legal ordinary
  reinforcement/combat/handoff actions for computer seats and no action for humans,
  eliminated players or finished games. Reinforce, attempt useful legal attacks,
  and reliably end turns; consume no RNG outside apply, preserving action-only
  replay. Add setup choice between existing all-human play and one human versus
  1–3 computers (2–4 total seats); existing default/routes remain compatible.
  Clearly identify computer seats and their active turn. Automate without requiring
  battle-dialog clicks; prevent human moves during computer turns while keeping
  Save/Load/New game usable. Resume mixed-seat saves, including computer-to-computer
  handoffs; cancel scheduled work on replacement/disposal/victory. Keep Normal
  metadata without claiming three implemented strengths. No dependencies or search.
