# T95 Grown islands: the v2 geography generator

- **Kind**: slice (0.2.0; owner, 2026-10-21: "the island shapes still suck").
- **Issue**: #181 · **Branch**: the session branch · **Merge after**: T94.
- **Review**: none; self-review in the session (#222 Q1). The owner judges the maps from screenshots.
- **Evidence**: #181 and the owner's answers there (2026-10-21): a new versioned generator; island count and sizes may vary per seed; 46 countries and the fairness rules stay; done now, before T81. The look follows the revision-7 sketches. Every numeric constant in `island-geography-v2.ts` is [designed]; the hit square and label spacing are v1's.
- **Owns**: src/core/island-geography-v2.ts (new); src/core/island-geography.ts (version type); src/core/geographic-board.ts, src/core/geographic-game.ts and src/core/match-save.ts (generator selection and the geographic/2 identities); src/ui/geographicGame.ts (new games use v2; `?generator=1` keeps v1); src/ui/generatedGeographyPresentation.ts (accepts v2); tests/core/island-geography-v2.test.ts; e2e specs that pin v1 maps request `generator=1`.
- **Scope**: Each island is the connected set of cells of a fine hex lattice inside a noisy, rotated ellipse. Strands narrower than three cells are trimmed and lakes filled. Its countries are a balanced geodesic partition with jittered costs, so they stay contiguous and organic. Outlines are traced on cell edges, and every shared chain is smoothed once, so neighbours keep identical borders. Each label gets v1's 40 px hit square and spacing. Sea lanes form a spanning tree over the islands, plus the next shortest pairs. Lanes are straight, clear of land and of each other, and at most two per country. They keep v1's bonus headroom, fortress and concentration rules. Saves record geographic/2; geographic/1 saves replay unchanged. The existing drawing pass then gives v2 its hand-drawn wobble.
- **Done when**:
  1. `npx vitest run tests/core/island-geography-v2.test.ts`: every corpus seed yields a valid map: 46 countries on 7–10 islands of 3–10, simple outlines, 40 px label squares and spacing, mutual borders, connected lanes clear of land with bonus headroom. The generator is deterministic and varied, and a v2 game and its save replay on v2.
  2. `npx playwright test tests/e2e/grown-islands.spec.ts --workers=2`: a new game is geographic/2. On the drawn map every country's 40 px square hits that country at both desktop sizes, and on a phone the turned map shows every country. Save and Load keep the map.
  3. `npm test && npm run build`: pass; CI's full browser suite is green, and geographic/1 tests and saves are unchanged.
- **Hazards**: Never change v1's output: its oracles and saves stay byte-exact. The core stays pure: integer arithmetic plus `Math.sqrt`, no trigonometry and no ambient randomness.
