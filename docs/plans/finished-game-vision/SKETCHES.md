regions. It is **not a demonstration of the required geographic variety**, a

## Reading and committing a decision

Every country retains its stack in a square/diamond/hexagonal/round badge for
blue/orange/lime/purple. Compact numbers are at least 14px. Where a schematic
region is too tight, a short leader joins the badge to its country. The list and
Focus remain alternatives for precise access; these images do not certify touch
hit areas. Inspection is optional for additional information, not required to
read armies. During placement the icon-plus-number pool survives hint dismissal.

Source selection immediately fills the source badge gold and uses a thick solid
outline. Legal enemy neighbours have a contrasting dashed outline; unrelated
countries dim. A directional arrow and different source/target badge treatments
make the staged pair clear. Selecting an own neighbour offers **Move armies** or
Cancel; it never attacks or commits a transfer automatically. Friendly movement
previews the amount and explicitly commits **Move & end turn**. Cancel returns
to attacks. This follows the existing movement contract, not a new rule.

Capture and movement previews use dashed count badges until confirmation.
Capture illustrates edited total 3 from seven survivors: 4 stay, 3 move, range
1–6. Direct entry, minus/plus and range adjustment remain. Fold preserves that
pending total; a distinct return icon resumes without confirming. Required
choices cannot timeout or be bypassed by End turn, reading or camera movement.

The [expanded teaching sequence](BEGINNER-WALKTHROUGH.md) shows source/target
selection, both casualty directions, explicit Attack, optional Fight on/Stop,
capture/fold/resume, human/opponent turns, help, movement and human elimination.
Card trade/Raid/event and private-handover details remain text-only in the journey
and existing contracts; the drawings do not claim to cover those flows visually.

## Distinct actions and coherent goals

End turn uses a flag; Fast-forward uses double chevrons. Fit has outward arrows;
Focus is a crosshair. Start game has a start-plus mark, Start combat a combat
symbol, and New game a new-page mark. Stop uses a hand, distinct from every owner
badge. First-use/requested names remain available by tap and keyboard focus.

Setup, late game and victory consistently illustrate default domination:
34 blue countries before reaching the 35-country goal, then a result with 35 of
46. Both ownership and displayed progress derive from one illustration state.
Elimination shows no human territories and offers Watch computers or Stop game;
recording is exactly once by contract, not proven by these drawings.

## Map, navigation and temporary surfaces

Portrait turns the same world 90°, including tablet portrait. Phone landscape,
tablet landscape and desktop use the unturned world. Counts and controls remain
upright. Fit always returns to the complete archipelago. Focus is separate;
pinch or requested +/− zooms, and swipe/drag pans when enlarged. Camera gestures
never become placement or Attack. Selection, pending total, world-space focus
and keyboard focus survive rotation. Compare capture-tablet with
capture-tablet-landscape: both retain the 3 total and 4 / 3 pending preview.

Wide active views temporarily reframe the same geography to keep contextual
cards in open sea; dismissing them restores the unobstructed map view. No
permanent space is reserved for a reading column. In focused phone views,
controls protect the focused island and its counts; Fit/pan/list access remains
available for peripheral cropped geography. Requested help, private rewards,
setup and result are deliberate overlays and can cover the map while open.
Production centres have offset neutral symbols and leaders, clear of count badges.

Native action icons are 48×48. Static labels and the shown viewports do not
replace supported-size, safe-area, browser zoom, enlarged-text, keyboard, privacy,
reduced-motion or real input checks. Original vivid regions, layered shorelines,
restrained contours and direct consequences maintain the requested Isle Wars Pro
bias without copying artwork, code or wording. Existing count/topology/fairness,
route, RNG, rule and save contracts remain unchanged.
